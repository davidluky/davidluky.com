## Generation prompts

Composition: pet central-right at medium scale, environment fills full width, foreground plants frame lower corners. Make a cohesive original promotional illustration, not a screenshot or UI mockup. No UI, no interface, no coins, no numbers, no text, no logos, no badges, no extra characters. Do not suggest unreleased gameplay. Preserve recognizable Flameko identity and cheerful gentle tone; no photorealism or 3D. This will be explicitly captioned as promotional illustration on the website.
